Privacy Policy — StorytimeKit Autopost

StorytimeKit Autopost is a private tool used only by the owner of the StorytimeKit shop to publish the shop's own product videos to the shop's own social media accounts.

It does not collect, store or share personal data from anyone else. It only uses access tokens for accounts the owner controls, and those tokens are kept as encrypted GitHub secrets.

Data deletion: there is no user data to delete. To revoke access, remove the app from your Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or Pinterest settings.

Contact: via the StorytimeKit Etsy shop.
